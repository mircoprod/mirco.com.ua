About the fund

Mir & Co Foundation

“Mir & Co” non-profit organization, listed among verified charitable structures, was founded in August 2014. In November 2014 the Foundation's work was recognized with a grant from the International Renaissance Foundation by George Soros. Starting from 2015, the Foundation co-operates with the “Nova Poshta” company, which launched the “Ukrainian Humanitarian Post” project — enabling volunteer organizations and initiative groups to send and receive humanitarian cargo free of charge at any office of the company nationwide.

“Mir & Co” foundation delivers food, ammunition, clothes, shoes, and equipment for Ukrainian military units and battalions. It also supplies medication to wounded soldiers in hospitals, and helps children in orphanages and internally displaced persons.
